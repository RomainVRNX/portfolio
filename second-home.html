Commissioned Photography / Moalboal, Philippines

Second Home

Second Home is a welcoming, well-equipped hostel in Moalboal, with a pool, bar, shared spaces and comfortable rooms. I had the opportunity to photograph the property, capturing both the spaces themselves and the atmosphere that makes the place feel like a home away from home. As my first commissioned photography project, it was a real pleasure to work with the team and bring this place to life through my images.

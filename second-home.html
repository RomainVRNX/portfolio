Commissioned Photography / Moalboal, Philippines

Second Home

The Project

Second Home, a newly opened hostel in Moalboal, Philippines, needed professional photographs on short notice for its launch.

I delivered a first selection of photographs of the rooms and key shared spaces within 24 hours. I delivered the remaining images within seven days, after scheduling a second shoot to capture the outdoor spaces in better natural light.
